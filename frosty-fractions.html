Frosty Fractions

Ten fraction-multiplying questions picked from a bank of 100, then build your own Gingerbread Kingdom: gather candy, bake gingerbread villagers, train elves and snowmen, call Santa, and chase away the grumpy Humbugs. Every right answer earns one minute of game time, and high scores bring gifts from Santa's sleigh. Your village is saved between rounds. Your progress is saved, so you can close the page and come back.

Tip: To multiply fractions, multiply the tops and multiply the bottoms, then simplify. "Of" means multiply. Type fractions like 3/8 and mixed numbers like 1 2/5.

Tap a villager or building to select it, then tap where to go, what to gather or who to bonk. Drag to look around (mouse: drag a box to select, right-drag or arrow keys to scroll).
