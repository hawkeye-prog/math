Division Quest

Answer 40 division questions. Get 36 or more right and the gate to Star Kingdom opens for 15 minutes of play.

How it works

  • You get 3 quests. Every quest has brand-new questions.
  • Some questions are multiple choice, some you type the number.
  • At the end you see your score and a fix-it list for anything you missed.
  • A score of 36 or better unlocks Star Kingdom, a run-and-jump game.
